# Compliance Certifications and Attestations

This document provides an overview of A2A's compliance certifications and attestations for procurement and security assessment purposes.

## Overview

A2A maintains rigorous compliance standards to ensure the security, privacy, and integrity of data and systems. This document outlines our certifications, compliance frameworks, and available attestation documentation.

## Certifications and Frameworks

### PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)

**Status**: PCI-DSS v4.0 Compliant

**Scope**: All systems handling, processing, or transmitting cardholder data

**Key Controls**:
- Network segmentation and firewall protection

- Strong access control measures with multi-factor authentication
- Regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing
- Comprehensive logging and monitoring of all access to cardholder data
- Annual on-site assessments by Qualified Security Assessor (QSA)

- Attestation of Compliance (AOC)
- Report on Compliance (ROC)
- Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) - Type D

- HITECH Act provisions

**Protected Health Information (PHI) Safeguards**:

**Administrative Safeguards**:
- Security management process with risk analysis and risk management
- Workforce security training and awareness programs
- Information access management with role-based access controls
- Security incident procedures and response protocols
- Contingency planning including disaster recovery and backup procedures
- Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all relevant third parties

**Physical Safeguards**:
- Facility access controls with authorized personnel only
- Workstation and device security policies
